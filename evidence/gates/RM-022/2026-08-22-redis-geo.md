  `UP`.

  courier UUID with latitude `31.2` and longitude `121.5` was inserted and
  read back from `routemind.courier_locations`.
- Authenticated Redis `GEOADD` followed by `GEOSEARCH ... BYRADIUS 10 km
  WITHDIST` returned the same courier UUID at distance `0.0001` km.
- The probe removed both the durable row and GEO key before infrastructure
  shutdown. Compose down preserved development volumes.
