# Claim Boundaries

## Permitted now

- Describe implemented interfaces, deterministic replay behavior, bounded
  local execution, and contract validation.
- Generate hypotheses and design preregistered exploratory experiments using
  synthetic or explicitly verified external fixtures.
- Report failures, partial results, no-data results, and reproducibility checks.

## Prohibited without new evidence and gates

- Production, live-provider, Tokyo-pinned processing, or cloud readiness claims.
- Twin fidelity, external validity, causal impact, OPE estimates, RADS safety,
  switching stability, policy-boundary generalization, or strategy superiority.
- Novelty/patent/thesis claims; RouteBench implementation is not novelty.
- Reinterpreting or rerunning frozen R3-325, Google R4-411B, VKE/VM/SSH, or
  other append-only external evidence.

Every future claim must link a new manifest, raw artifacts, environment and
dataset lineage, exact analysis, uncertainty, independent verification, and the
central claim matrix. Unsupported fields must be reported as unavailable.
