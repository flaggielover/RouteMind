# RouteMind Science Context

RouteMind studies dispatch, optimization, simulation, risk-aware policy
selection, and reproducible operational experiments. Java owns durable business
state and transactions; Python owns dispatch, optimization, Digital Twin,
RouteBench, RADS, analytics, and experiments. PostgreSQL is durable truth;
Redis is rebuildable hot state.

## Artifact layout

Code, schemas, small fixtures, manifests, scripts, and summaries belong in the
repository. Large datasets, experiment outputs, caches, and logs belong below
`ROUTEMIND_DATA_ROOT` (intended local path `F:\Projects\RouteMind-Data`).
Secrets never belong in Git, logs, screenshots, or evidence.

## How to use this context

Read `SCIENCE_READINESS.md` before proposing a study, then use
`EXPERIMENT_INTERFACE.md` to build a bounded manifest. Use
`CLAIM_BOUNDARIES.md` and `KNOWN_NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md` to prevent claim drift.
Candidates in `RESEARCH_CANDIDATES.md` are proposals only; they are not novelty
or effectiveness claims.
