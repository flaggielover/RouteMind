# Research Candidates

This file records candidate studies only. No entry is a novelty, causal,
superiority, safety, fidelity, or production claim.

| Candidate | State | Required evidence before a claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RADS-H | Scaffold | Tick-level switch/dwell/service/cost logs, paired CRN outcomes, uncertainty, and frozen statistical analysis. |
| Safe-RADS | Scaffold | Constraint violations, feasibility, calibration, lateness, route cost, and sensitivity outcomes with at least the frozen support. |
| Self-Calibrating Digital Twin | Scaffold | Disjoint observed calibration and held-out records, calibration fit, drift, and fidelity report. |
| Decision X-Ray | Scaffold | Executable captured state/policy bundle, same-model replay, minimal perturbation, deltas, and lineage. |
| Policy Boundary Learning | Scaffold | At least two supported stability cells and at least 30 records per strategy class, with uncertainty and sensitivity. |
| Multi-Agent Tradeoff Analysis | Candidate only | New study contract defining agents, objectives, interference, and non-authority boundaries. |

All candidates must use a new manifest version, preserve raw run-level outputs,
retain failures and exclusions, and pass the applicable Science Entry Gates.
